# Reporting issues

Provide package/plugin version, game runtime, F4SE version, the plugin startup/fallback log, relevant F4SE log excerpt, body/outfit versions and shape/SSF setup, skeleton/physics configuration, other shader/engine plugins, and concise reproduction steps. Say whether the correction was actually activated or fell back to native processing. Screenshots can help distinguish a cut problem from ordinary blood effects.

Use the experimental package label for 221 reports. Do not describe 221 as gameplay-verified from a successful startup or mapped-PE test. Do not upload copyrighted game archives or third-party body assets publicly. No further 240 gameplay testing is required merely to repeat the project's accepted validation.
